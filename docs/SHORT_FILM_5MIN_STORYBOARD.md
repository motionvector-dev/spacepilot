# 5-Minute Cinematic Short Film: "An Unexpected Encounter"
## Production Storyboard & 10-Second Batch Prompt Sheet (LTX-Video Format)

- **Total Duration:** 5 minutes (300 seconds)
- **Batch Architecture:** 30 sequential 10-second clips (241 frames @ 24 FPS each)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1280×720 or 768×512 base)
- **Target Aesthetic:** 35mm film grain, 5600K golden hour transitioning to 4000K twilight, photorealistic cinema.

---

### Production Overview & Act Structure

```text
┌──────────────┬─────────────┬──────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Act / Phase  │ Timestamps  │ Shots        │ Dramatic Purpose                          │
├──────────────┼─────────────┼──────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Act 1        │ 0:00 – 1:00 │ Shots 01–06  │ The Inciting Meeting in a Rooftop Café    │
│ Act 2A       │ 1:00 – 2:30 │ Shots 07–15  │ Dialogue, Shared Humor & Connection       │
│ Act 2B       │ 2:30 – 3:40 │ Shots 16–22  │ Evening Stroll & Rising Tension           │

└──────────────┴─────────────┴──────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
