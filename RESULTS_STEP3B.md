## 4. Key Architectural Insights

1. **Unconstrained Routing Drift:** In standard unrestricted Qwen inference, the router rapidly rotates through experts across a 32-token window (touching ~42 out of 60 experts per layer). Passively observing only the first 4 tokens captures **24.0% of all experts** that unrestricted decoding will eventually wander through.
2. **Passive Prediction is Insufficient for Unconstrained Generation:** You cannot accurately predict the full unconstrained expert path 32–64 tokens ahead solely by observing 1–4 early tokens without prefetching a much larger candidate pool ($K \ge 32-48$).
3. **The Synergistic Fix (Constrained Candidate Pool):** While unconstrained generation wanders, Step 1/2 proved that *constraining* decode to a prefetched candidate pool retains **>94.7% gating mass** and doubles speed to **76–88 tok/s**. Therefore, prefetching must be coupled with constrained decoding rather than unconstrained passive tracking.

---

## 5. Final Decision Verdict

# **`PROMISING`**

### Summary:
* **Passive Predictor Alone:** `FAILED` for unconstrained generation (only 24%–38% recall over 32 tokens).
* **System Architecture as a Whole:** `PROMISING` when combined with **Constrained Pool Routing** (Step 1/2), where early token sampling determines the candidate pool and the model's decode is locked to that candidate pool for the next 16–32 tokens.
